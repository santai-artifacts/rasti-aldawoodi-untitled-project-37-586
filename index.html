About

A bit about me

I've spent the last several years at the intersection of design and engineering — the messy, interesting place where good ideas become real things people use.

My approach is simple: understand the problem deeply, prototype quickly, and sweat the details until it feels right. I believe great products come from tight feedback loops, honest conversations, and a genuine respect for the people on the other side of the screen.

When I'm not shipping, you'll find me sketching interfaces, reading about typography, or chasing the perfect cup of coffee.
